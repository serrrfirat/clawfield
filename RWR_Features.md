# Running With Rifles - Relevant Gameplay Features for Clawfield

> Analysis of RWR mechanics that could enhance Clawfield's top-down tactical shooter gameplay.

## Core Combat Mechanics

### Weapons & Ballistics
- **Iron sights** - Hold right-click to aim down sights, reduces spread, increases accuracy
- **Recoil control** - Weapon sway that increases with sustained fire
- **Suppression mechanic** - Enemies in fire zones get "suppressed" (screen blur, accuracy penalty)
- **Weapon jam/malfunction** - Random chance after sustained fire requiring "fix" action
- **Weapon overheating** - Continuous fire causes overheating, must wait to cool down
- **Bipods** - Deployable bipod for prone/weapon rest (reduced recoil)

### Movement
- **Sprinting** - Hold shift to sprint (limited stamina)
- **Prone stance** - Go prone for reduced profile but slower movement
- **Crouching** - Intermediate stance with balance between speed and stealth
- **Lean** - Q/E to lean around corners without exposing full body
- **Vaulting** - Automatic obstacle vaulting when sprinting into low cover

### Class System (from ET/Quake Wars heritage)
- **Soldier** - Heavy weapons, explosives, ammo crates
- **Medic** - Health packs, revive downed teammates
- **Engineer** - Repairs, deployable cover/barriers, landmines
- **Field Ops** - Air strikes, artillery, supply drops
- **Covert Ops** - Binoculars, covert ops, plant explosives

## Objective & Teamplay

### Capture Points
- **Neutral → Contested → Owned** - Multi-stage capture logic
- **Capture speed varies by number of players** - More teammates = faster capture
- **Defenders can interrupt capture** - Kill attackers on point to pause capture
- **Reinforcement timer** - Team respawns faster when owning more points

### Spawn System
- **Deployable spawn points** - Engineers can place spawn beacons
- **Forward spawns** - Spawn closer to action, risky but faster re-engagement
- **Spawn protection** - Brief invulnerability after spawning

### Commander/Team Leader
- **Artillery strikes** - Call in explosive bombardments
- **Air strikes** - Strafing runs on target areas
- **Supply drops** - Drop ammo/health crates
- **Radars** - Reveal enemy positions temporarily

## Progression & Economy

### XP & Levels
- **Skill points per class** - Earn XP per class separately
-- **Unlockable weapons/abilities** - Higher level = more options
- **Weapon proficiency** - Per-weapon XP tracks familiarity

### Resource Management
- **Limited ammo** - Must visit ammo crates/packs to resupply
- **Grenade inventory** - Limited grenades, must scavenge or get from supply
- **Health packs** - Limited uses, must find more or wait for medic

## Environmental

### Destruction
- **Destructible cover** - Some walls/objects can be destroyed
- **Fire propagation** - Explosions can set things on fire

### Vehicles (future consideration)
- **Driver/Gunner seats** - Multi-person vehicle operation
- **Vehicle damage** - Can disable vehicles, not just destroy
- **Crew roles** - Driver, commander, gunner positions

## Prioritized Recommendations for Clawfield

### High Priority (Now)
1. **Iron sights** - Right-click aim for better accuracy
2. **Suppression** - Force enemies to duck/retreat
3. **Ammo management** - Limited magazines, resupply mechanics
4. **Grenade types** - More variety (flash, HE, smoke)

### Medium Priority (Soon)
5. **Class system** - Distinct roles with unique abilities
6. **Deployable spawns** - Strategic spawn point placement
7. **Objective capture stages** - Multi-stage point control

### Lower Priority (Later)
8. **XP/leveling** - Progression system
9. **Commander abilities** - Call-ins for team play
10. **Vehicle system** - Crew-served vehicles
